# Contributing to the Extra-Atmospheric and Celestial Jurisdiction Code (EACJC)

Thank you for your interest in refining the legal frameworks of off-world jurisdictions. We welcome statutory submissions, regulatory integrations, and procedural updates from sovereign space delegates, corporate entities, and legal scholars.

## How to Submit Amendments

1.  **Fork the Repository**: Create a personal copy of this framework to test your proposed amendments.
2.  **Verify Definitions**: Ensure all terminology additions align with the lexicographical baselines established in Section 1 (Webster's 1828 Dictionary criteria).
3.  **Map Terrestrial Equivalents**: New criminal or civil codes must explicitly map back to an earthly sovereign structural mechanism (e.g., US Art. III, Canadian Sec. 101, or Australian Chapter III frameworks).
4.  **Open a Pull Request**: Submit your changes back to the `main` branch with a clear summary detailing the specific statutory baseline impact.

## Regulatory Integration Guidelines

*   **Zero Legal Vacuums**: Proposed changes must not create jurisdictional dead-zones inside life-support biomes.
*   **Human Dignity & Restorative Focus**: Align all operational employee adjustments with the baseline metrics established under the Section 4 restorative framework.
*   **Cryptographic & Formatting Integrity**: Maintain Markdown formatting integrity for tabular data arrays and structural maps.

All approved amendments will be aggregated into the next official framework version baseline.
