## Preamble

**WHEREAS** the expansion of human industry, commerce, and tourism into extra-atmospheric gravity spaces and celestial bodies necessitates a singular, unfragmented rule of law;

**WHEREAS** the traditional boundaries of terrestrial nations do not inherently extend to the void of space, risking legal vacuums that threaten human survival, corporate investment, and international peace;

**WHEREAS** the maintenance of life-support biomes and the preservation of orbital pathways free from destructive debris require absolute obedience to safety protocols, making traditional territorial disputes a direct threat to the collective existence of all spacefarers;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, under the executive direction of the Australian Space Agency delegation and in partnership with Aerochome, this Extra-Atmospheric and Celestial Jurisdiction Code (EACJC) is enacted. It binds all Sojourners and Servants to a unified judicial structure, fusing the apex legal traditions of Earth into a cohesive orbital framework grounded in restorative justice, corporate compliance, environmental preservation, and existential security.

## Section 1: Definition of Terms

In alignment with historical jurisprudential continuity, all legal terminology herein is defined in accordance with English lexicographical standards derived from the **Websters 1828 Dictionary**—the foundational authority utilized in the codification and interpretation of Ecclesiastical and Canon Law traditions.

*   **Jurisdiction**: The legal power or authority of a court to hear and determine a cause; the limit within which power may be exercised. In this Code, it extends beyond terrestrial boundaries to any vessel, station, or celestial body.
*   **Malice**: A formed design of doing mischief to another, called *malitia praecogitata*, or malice prepense or aforethought. It is the essential criterion distinguishing murder from manslaughter.
*   **Crime**: An act committed or omitted, in violation of a public law, either forbidding or commanding it. A crime is a wrong committed against the peace and dignity of the sovereign authority.
*   **Punishment**: Any pain, penalty, or forfeiture inflicted upon a person by due course of law, for a crime or offense, commanded by a court of justice. Its object is to prevent future offenses.
*   **Correction**: The act of punishing for the sake of amendment, or the discipline which is intended to change the course of a wrongdoer and restore them to a state of obedience.
*   **Servant (Employee)**: A person who owes subjection to another, and performs services for them, whether for a specified time or permanently, under a contract of employment or duty.
*   **Sojourner (Tourist)**: A temporary resident; a person who dwells or travels in a place for a time, possessing no permanent domicile or ownership of the station or vessel.
*   **Abjuration**: The act of renouncing, disclaiming, or forswearing upon oath; a solemn banishment or self-imposed exile from a specific jurisdiction or community.

## Section 2: Jurisdictional Integration of Terrestrial Court Systems

To prevent jurisdictional vacuums in extra-atmospheric gravity spaces, this Code establishes a consolidated **Orbital Federal Judiciary Hierarchy**. This structure adapts and maps directly to the apex judicial institutions of Earth's leading federal and regional systems.
